A little about me

Iian Kehn.
Ideas into action.

I’m the creator behind CORE, Acute Web, and Slate. My work brings together independent technology coverage and practical apps for everyday use.

I’m interested in how technology fits into our lives: what makes it useful, where it can improve, and how to give people more control over their own experience.

What I’m building

CORE is my technology publication. Acute Web is my browser project, with privacy at its center. Slate R1 focuses on notes and documents, while Slate R2 is a separate project for full word processing.

R1 and R2 serve different purposes and are developed independently.
